# AGENTS.md

This file provides guidance to Codex (Codex.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Project

A personal website (hobby project, not client work). Content will mix: about-me/bio, a resume/CV page derived from the owner's actual CV and LinkedIn profile, a projects/portfolio section, links to socials, and eventually a blog.

Design goal: deliberately non-standard/non-templated. Avoid shipping a component library's out-of-the-box look (Aceternity/Motion Primitives/etc. read as "generic AI-hackathon site" when used wholesale) — favor a hand-picked color/type palette plus one or two restrained, deliberate interactions over stacking every available effect.

## Planned stack

- **Astro + Tailwind** — chosen for its plugin/theme ecosystem, near-zero shipped JS by default, and first-class free hosting (Vercel/Netlify/Cloudflare Pages).
- **CV/LinkedIn → content**: [JSON Resume](https://jsonresume.org) schema, populated via a LinkedIn-export converter (e.g. `linkedin-to-jsonresume`), rendered as a page and exportable to PDF.
- Reference starters worth borrowing structure/ideas from (not templates to clone wholesale): [astrofy](https://github.com/manuelernestog/astrofy), [astro-portfolio-starter](https://github.com/drehimself/astro-portfolio-starter).

### Design concept (v4)

- **Palette "Stahl + Sonnengelb"** (tokens in `src/styles/global.css`): steel paper `--bg` #f4f6f8 (page and top bar), white `--paper` for raised surfaces (skill boxes, figure panels), ink `--ink` #0f1b2d, `--ink-2` #4f5d6e secondary text, `--steel` #7d8b99 for mirrors/ghost lines (never text), `--line` hairlines, `--ink-3` #64707d stepped-back text (4.66:1), `--wash` #e7ebf0 quiet bands, `--steel-soft` #dde3ea neutral fill (selection, AM lead area), **sun `--sun` #f2a900**. Sun yellow means **light, and nothing else**: the hero's sun, rays and receiver glow, the AM break-even dot, the heliostat schematic's sun. Nav, links, hovers, chips stay ink (a blunt critic caught yellow spreading to nine uses, the v2 "one accent everywhere" failure; keep it scarce). Fill only, always with ink text or an ink outline on it. `--ph` magenta stays outside the palette for placeholders.
- **Fixed light theme only**, deliberately **no** `prefers-color-scheme: dark` override (owner rejected white-on-dark).
- **Type**: one Google Fonts `<link>` (avoid `@fontsource`: subpath-export bug, likely the space in `personal website`). **Schibsted Grotesk** for UI; `.display` = 800 with tight tracking, **only** for the name, page titles and the Kontakt e-mail. Section heads (Kernkompetenzen, Software, Zertifikate, Aus der Praxis, Neben dem Job) = Schibsted 700 ~1.25rem, quiet. **Newsreader** 600 for content headings (station titles, essay and practice titles, skill box titles) and `.text` for prose, claim, stories. (The critic found every heading in heavy grotesk again, so hierarchy collapsed to size alone; don't spread `.display`.) Careful: Schibsted's `tabular-nums` (`.tnum`) also widens `,` and `.` to digit width ("1 . 566"), so use `.tnum` only on digit-only strings (years, slider outputs), never on prose with decimals.
- **Structure lines**: the top bar, the tab bar and section/essay breaks are closed by one 1.5px ink rule; hairlines inside.
- **Hover-to-trace** (owner liked it): pointing at something picks it out together with what it links to, with an **ink** outline/bar (not sun): Werdegang chart bar ↔ station, skill name → the rows of every station that fed that skill. Links hover to a thicker ink underline.
- **Layout**: every page has the identical top bar (name, nav with an ink underline for the current page at ≥64rem, "Lebenslauf (PDF)" on every page); sticky on phones (height `--top-h`), static at ≥64rem. Below 64rem the nav is the fixed bottom **tab bar** (`TabBar.astro`, ink bar on the current tab). `html` has `scroll-padding-top/bottom` for both bars.
- **Pages**:
  - Übersicht: **heliostat field hero** (`components/figures/HeliostatField.astro` + `lib/heliostat.ts`): side view, pointer (or finger) = sun, every mirror's normal bisects sun and receiver directions. Three rows for depth (`ROWS`: back rows smaller, higher, lighter). Incoming rays = thin dashed steel, front row only (all rows made a moiré fan); reflected rays in sun, opacity by the mirror's cosine (`rayOpacity`). Receiver glow cubic in `fieldEfficiency` = mean cosine × sun height, so a low sun leaves it dim; a sun behind the tower is deliberately *not* dimmed (physically the best cosine, which is why real fields sit opposite the sun). Without a pointer the sun drifts along a flat day arc, kept inside the visible slice and right of the text (computed from the SVG's CTM on resize/font load); not with reduced motion; paused off-screen. `SUN_START` {1165, 84} clears the text at every width. SVG `preserveAspectRatio="xMaxYMax slice"`, tower on the right. At ≥48rem the name (huge, two lines) and role sit in the field's sky; on phones the text stacks above a 17rem field strip (overlaying collided with the cropped tower). No-JS = the server-rendered picture at `SUN_START`. Under the ground line: the claim (serif, marked AI, max 22em, `text-wrap: pretty`) + the portrait (square, 1.5px ink frame). No explanatory caption (the critic: "if it needs a manual, the affordance is failing"). Then the **skill hub** (owner request 2026-09-25: 4 real boxes, 2×2, white, 1px hairline frame that turns ink on hover, no coloured edge, no shadow; body = line icon + title + proof lines; footer = centred software logos with names, "Gelernt bei" line; subgrid so footers align; `titleBreaks` soft hyphens; logos and licences in `src/assets/logos/`; dashed magenta monograms = logo placeholders; `toolsQuestion` asks the owner instead of guessing). Ends on a wash band with the e-mail. The home `<meta name="description">` stays neutral and factual, never the AI claim.
  - Werdegang: **project plan as a swimlane Gantt** (owner, 2026-09-28/29; he rejected a heliostat/light metaphor here, and after a first one-row-per-station Gantt asked for grouped categories on the left, more colour, different milestones and fuller stations). A sticky chart (under the top bar below 64rem, top 0 above): lanes Ausbildung / Abschlussarbeiten / Praxis on the left (swatch, name, subtitle; on phones a small name above each lane), one colour per category (`--c-edu` #2f55c7, `--c-thesis` #c98500, `--c-work` #0e9384 in global.css, owner's pick "Kobalt / Senf / Petrol", validated with the dataviz palette checker; Senf is never text). Stations are coloured bars with their full name above (`short ?? title`), **packed client-side from measured label widths** (bold weight) on load/font load/resize so labels never overlap or truncate; the server packs from an estimate as the no-JS fallback, which only holds at ≥80rem, so without JS the chart is hidden below that. Chronological DOM order per lane (Tab order). Milestones = filled ink **flags** hanging from the bar with a short name (`career[].milestone.tag`); a bar ends at its flag when the milestone is in its last ~1.5 months. Year grid, dashed "heute" (hidden near the playhead tag), labels on a `--bg` backing above the lines. **States:** at rest a full-strength plan, no playhead; while reading, the station at the reading line is ringed in ink, later ones pale, a playhead + date tag moves in real time between station starts; only story/tasks of other stations dim (`--ink-3`); at the page end every station running "heute" is current. Peek (hover/focus, or a skill name below) = dashed outline. Pins from bar clicks, deep links, hashchange/popstate (with `scrollRestoration = "manual"`, the station is scrolled into place) and focus, released by any other scroll; focusing a bar never scrolls. Where the last station plus the end band don't fit under the pinned chart, the chart is pushed off at the end and fades out (never shown half-cut). **Stations:** 3 columns ≥64rem: meta (category swatch + name, dates, org) | a short colour bar above the serif title, intro + task list (`tasks`, AI-drafted, in a Placeholder), a milestone box (flag, label, date; always the middle column's width), the heliostat schematic for the Bachelorarbeit (max 24rem), "Kernkompetenzen" | facts table ("Dauer" computed for jobs and theses, plus `facts`), "Software" logos (`tools`, tools.ts; Jedox/Timebutler/Copilot are marked monogram placeholders), an image placeholder (`visual`). Minor stations use the same grid with a smaller title. Phones: title → meta → main → side.
  - Projekte: page title + a plain index of the four projects (anchor links), then essays (Newsreader 600 titles, italic serif byline after the title, serif prose, "Ergebnis." run-in), separated by an ink rule, the second on a wash band. Master thesis: prose → **life-cycle figure** (`figures/LifeCycle.astro`: no card, opens with an ink rule; two plain bars on one scale, real numbers from the thesis, Tabellen 25/26: use phase ~99 % of the energy, hollow variant ~68 % less) → result → the **AM model** (`figures/AmModel.astro` + `lib/amModel.ts`, unchanged mechanics: a simplified titanium example with rounded literature values, now framed as "for metal, production matters; try it yourself", explicitly *not* thesis data). Heliostat schematic beside the text from 75rem.
  - Kenntnisse: ledger (level words as row heads, skill names large serif), 3fr/2fr grid; "Kernkompetenzen und wo sie herkommen" is one column with the "Gelernt bei" links right under each skill; ink gutter bar on `:target` only (no hover).
  - Kontakt: letterhead (address block all Schibsted, name 700), huge e-mail in the heavy grotesk with a steel underline, CV button (ink, `--ink-2` on hover), "Neben dem Job".
- **Data encoding**: Beruf = solid ink, Ausbildung = ink outline on white (no hatching, owner found it ugly). Levels only where the CV self-rates them (`ratedSkills`); never invent one.
- **Avoid the generated-site tells**: no uppercase tracked eyebrows, no mono micro-labels, no KPI stat tiles (incl. "big word + small caption"), no "→" appended to links, no middle-dot meta strings, sparing em dashes, no numbered "how it works" steps, no repeated meta-line → bold title → paragraph → link recipe. Copy: plain, specific German; no aphorisms or filler; never invent facts or figures.

- **Placeholders for AI-drafted content** (owner request 2026-09-25): every text, number or proposal an AI wrote (not taken from CV/LinkedIn/theses) is wrapped in `components/Placeholder.astro` (`kind`: text / data / vorschlag; block = tag + dashed magenta rule hanging **in the gutter**; `inline` = dashed underline + "KI" mark with a short sr-only "(KI-Text)"). Spots where a real picture would help use `components/ImagePlaceholder.astro` (`role="img"`, label and note in the `aria-label`). A magenta draft notice sits at the top of every page (Layout.astro). The header comment in `cv.ts` lists which fields are AI drafts (`// KI-PLATZHALTER`). When the owner replaces a text, remove its wrapper; when all are gone, remove the draft notice. Pure interface copy (labels, the hero's one-line explanation, the Werdegang lede) is not wrapped.

### Architecture

- `src/data/cv.ts` — all CV/LinkedIn/thesis content as typed data: `profile` (incl. `claim`), `career` (each with `story`; `from`/`to` as `"2020"`, `"Okt 2021"` or `"heute"`; optional `minor` and `project` slug), `projects` (ordered by weight; `slug` = anchor, optional `figure`), `ratedSkills`, `competencies`, `otherCompetencies`, `certifications` (with `id` anchors), `notes`, `topSkills` (home hub, Kenntnisse grouping, Werdegang chips: `id`, `title`, `proof[]`, `tools[]`, optional `toolsQuestion`, `learned[]`). `career[].id` and `certifications[].id` are link targets, keep them stable. Edit content here, not in components. Exceptions: the AM model's essay text lives in `AmModel.astro`, the life-cycle numbers in `LifeCycle.astro` (both written around their figure).
- `src/data/nav.ts` — the 5 routes (top bar and tab bar, incl. tab icons) and the CV PDF path. `src/data/tools.ts` — software logos for the hub.
- `src/lib/dates.ts` — `toFrac()` turns `from`/`to` strings into fractional years (year-only starts count as September, ends as July; "heute" = build date, so rebuild to move it).
- `src/lib/heliostat.ts` — hero field geometry (`FIELD`, `MIRROR_X`, `SUN_START`, `poses()`, `clampSun()`, `fieldEfficiency()`), shared by server render and client script.
- `src/lib/amModel.ts` — AM model constants, presets, `amModel()`, `amMarks()`, `amSvg()`, `amVerdict()`, formatters.
- `src/components/` — `TabBar`, `Placeholder`, `ImagePlaceholder`, `figures/HeliostatField`, `figures/Heliostat` (reflection schematic on Projekte), `figures/LifeCycle`, `figures/AmModel`.
- `src/layouts/Layout.astro` — shell: fonts, `html.js` one-liner, skip link, draft notice, top bar, `<main id="inhalt" class="content">` with bottom padding for the tab bar, tab bar. Pages wrap their own content in `.wrap` so bands can go full-bleed.
- `src/pages/index.astro` (Übersicht), `werdegang/`, `projekte/`, `kenntnisse/`, `kontakt/`.
- JS on the site: the `html.js` one-liner, the hero field, the Werdegang scroll sync, the AM model. Everything works without it.
- Page heads: top padding 3rem at ≥64rem on the four inner pages.
- CSS cascade caution: media-query overrides must come *after* the base rule they override (this broke the site twice); page-level overrides of layout/global rules need higher specificity (e.g. `:global(:root)`, `:global(main.content#inhalt)`) because Astro's scoped selectors carry an extra attribute.
