# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Project

A personal website (hobby project, not client work). Content will mix: about-me/bio, a resume/CV page derived from the owner's actual CV and LinkedIn profile, a projects/portfolio section, links to socials, and eventually a blog.

Design goal: deliberately non-standard/non-templated. Avoid shipping a component library's out-of-the-box look (Aceternity/Motion Primitives/etc. read as "generic AI-hackathon site" when used wholesale) — favor a hand-picked color/type palette plus one or two restrained, deliberate interactions over stacking every available effect.

## Planned stack

- **Astro + Tailwind** — chosen for its plugin/theme ecosystem, near-zero shipped JS by default, and first-class free hosting (Vercel/Netlify/Cloudflare Pages).
- **CV/LinkedIn → content**: [JSON Resume](https://jsonresume.org) schema, populated via a LinkedIn-export converter (e.g. `linkedin-to-jsonresume`), rendered as a page and exportable to PDF.
- Reference starters worth borrowing structure/ideas from (not templates to clone wholesale): [astrofy](https://github.com/manuelernestog/astrofy), [astro-portfolio-starter](https://github.com/drehimself/astro-portfolio-starter).

### Design concept (v4)

- **Palette "Stahl + Sonnengelb"** (tokens in `src/styles/global.css`): steel paper `--bg` #f4f6f8 (page and top bar), white `--paper` for raised surfaces (skill boxes, figure panels), ink `--ink` #0f1b2d, `--ink-2` #4f5d6e secondary text, `--steel` #7d8b99 for mirrors/ghost lines (never text), `--line` hairlines, `--ink-3` #64707d stepped-back text (4.66:1), `--wash` #e7ebf0 quiet bands, `--steel-soft` #dde3ea neutral fill (selection, AM lead area), **sun `--sun` #f2a900**. Sun yellow means **light, and nothing else**: the hero's sun, rays and receiver glow, the AM break-even dot, the heliostat schematic's sun. Nav, links, hovers, chips stay ink (a blunt critic caught yellow spreading to nine uses, the v2 "one accent everywhere" failure; keep it scarce). Fill only, always with ink text or an ink outline on it. `--ph` magenta stays outside the palette for placeholders.
- **Fixed light theme only**, deliberately **no** `prefers-color-scheme: dark` override (owner rejected white-on-dark).
- **Type**: one Google Fonts `<link>` (avoid `@fontsource`: subpath-export bug, likely the space in `personal website`). **Schibsted Grotesk** for UI; `.display` = 800 with tight tracking, **only** for page titles and the Kontakt e-mail. The hero name is **Newsreader 500** (owner, 2026-10-01: the heavy grotesk looked clunky; picked from mockups in `.superpowers/brainstorm/hero-name-fonts.html`). Section heads (Software, Zertifikate, Neben dem Job) = Schibsted 700 ~1.25rem, quiet. **Newsreader** 600 for content headings (station titles, essay and practice titles, skill box titles) and `.text` for prose, claim, stories. (The critic found every heading in heavy grotesk again, so hierarchy collapsed to size alone; don't spread `.display`.) Careful: Schibsted's `tabular-nums` (`.tnum`) also widens `,` and `.` to digit width ("1 . 566"), so use `.tnum` only on digit-only strings (years, slider outputs), never on prose with decimals.
- **Structure lines**: the top bar and the section breaks (`.sec` in global.css: Werdegang, Kenntnisse, Kontakt; the hero is closed by the field's ground line) are one 1.5px ink rule; hairlines inside.
- **Hover-to-trace** (owner liked it): pointing at something picks it out together with what it links to, with an **ink** outline/bar (not sun): Werdegang chart bar ↔ station, skill name → the rows of every station that fed that skill. Links hover to a thicker ink underline.
- **Layout**: one page. Top bar = name (links to the top), nav of in-page anchors (`#kompetenzen`, `#werdegang`, `#kenntnisse`, `#kontakt`) with an ink bar on the section in view (Layout.astro: the last section whose top passed a third of the window, by position, since Werdegang's last station sits outside its section box; colour + bar only, no bold), "Lebenslauf (PDF)"; sticky on phones (height `--top-h`, nav hidden there: name + CV only), static at ≥64rem. **No tab bar** any more. Old URLs (`/werdegang/`, `/kenntnisse/`, `/kontakt/`, `/projekte/`) are noindex redirect pages (`components/Redirect.astro`) to the same place on the one page, keeping the hash (project anchors map to their stations). Werdegang's `scrollRestoration = "manual"` now covers the whole page, so it restores its own saved `wdY` on a reload or Back without a hash. `html` has `scroll-padding-top` for the top bar.
- **Pages**:
  - Übersicht: **heliostat field hero** (`components/figures/HeliostatField.astro` + `lib/heliostat.ts`): side view, pointer (or finger) = sun, every mirror's normal bisects sun and receiver directions. Three rows for depth (`ROWS`: back rows smaller, higher, lighter). Incoming rays = thin dashed steel, front row only (all rows made a moiré fan); reflected rays in sun, opacity by the mirror's cosine (`rayOpacity`). Receiver glow cubic in `fieldEfficiency` = mean cosine × sun height, so a low sun leaves it dim; a sun behind the tower is deliberately *not* dimmed (physically the best cosine, which is why real fields sit opposite the sun). Without a pointer the sun drifts along a flat day arc, kept inside the visible slice and right of the text (computed from the SVG's CTM on resize/font load); not with reduced motion; paused off-screen. `SUN_START` {1165, 84} clears the text at every width. SVG `preserveAspectRatio="xMaxYMax slice"`, tower on the right; the viewBox starts at `FIELD.x0` = -480 (empty ground) and ends just under the ground line (`h` 502), so the field draws smaller (owner, 2026-10-01) and the ground line itself closes the hero (the hub has no top rule). At ≥48rem the text block sets the hero height and the SVG sits at the bottom at its full 1920:502 aspect (scale follows page width only); name (huge, two lines), role and the claim (serif, quoted, width capped so it ends left of the mirrors at ~46vw) sit in the sky and the text block stands on the ground line like the field (owner: "same level"); on phones the text stacks above a 13rem field strip (overlaying collided with the cropped tower). No-JS = the server-rendered picture at `SUN_START`. The portrait is **blended into the sky in the middle, between the text and the field** (owner, 2026-10-01; replaced the old claim + framed-portrait box; rejected along the way: blended behind the name, framed beside the name): `mix-blend-mode: multiply` + radial mask, `z-index: -1` behind the rays (`.field-wrap` is `isolation: isolate` on a `--bg` backing so the multiply still hits the paper); on phones between the claim and the field strip. The sun's drift range keeps clear of it. No explanatory caption (the critic: "if it needs a manual, the affordance is failing"). Then **Kernkompetenzen as stacking panels** (owner 2026-10-01, after a swipe carousel, a sideways row and a 2×2 grid; scroll effect after marcuslorenzet.com/#featured): the four names are the heading (`.kk-title`, Newsreader 500, names as links to `#kk-<id>`, commas in `--ink-3`; replaced the banner "Diese vier Bereiche zeichnen mich aus."). One panel per skill: with JS each `.panel` is sticky with no background of its own (top `--stick`, set by the script: the card pinned centred in the space under the top bar, or pinned by its bottom edge if taller than that), so the incoming card itself slides over the pinned one, its edge a 1.5px ink top rule; the covered card steps back (scale to 0.92) and fades out completely (nothing with reduced motion). Focus in a covered card (Shift+Tab) scrolls back to where that panel has just pinned; without JS they simply follow each other. Card = text on white paper (title Newsreader 600, two short proof lines, then small logos + "Gelernt bei" folded into the text under a hairline, then a real link "Weiter: <next>" with a down arrow icon, the last one to Werdegang) beside an **uncovered photo** (≥56rem: text 2fr | photo 3fr with a 1.5px ink rule between; phones: photo 16:10 on top) in **one treatment for all**: greyscale multiplied onto `--steel-soft`. No white-on-dark veil any more. Placeholder photos = magenta stripes with `imageIdea`. - **Avoid the generated-site tells**: no uppercase tracked eyebrows, no mono micro-labels, no KPI stat tiles (incl. "big word + small caption"), no "→" appended to links, no middle-dot meta strings, sparing em dashes, no numbered "how it works" steps, no repeated meta-line → bold title → paragraph → link recipe. Copy: plain, specific German; no aphorisms or filler; never invent facts or figures.

### Architecture

- `src/data/cv.ts` — all CV/LinkedIn/thesis content as typed data: `profile` (incl. `claim`), `career` (each with `story`; `from`/`to` as `"2020"`, `"Okt 2021"` or `"heute"`; optional `minor` and `project` slug), `projects` (ordered by weight; `slug` = anchor, optional `figure`), `ratedSkills`, `competencies`, `otherCompetencies`, `certifications` (with `id` anchors), `notes`, `topSkills` (home hub, Kenntnisse grouping, Werdegang chips: `id`, `title`, `proof[]`, `tools[]`, optional `toolsQuestion`, `learned[]`). `career[].id` and `certifications[].id` are link targets, keep them stable. Edit content here, not in components. Exceptions: the AM model's essay text lives in `AmModel.astro`, the life-cycle numbers in `LifeCycle.astro` (both written around their figure).
- `src/data/nav.ts` — the in-page anchors for the top bar and the CV PDF path. `src/data/tools.ts` — software logos.
- `src/lib/dates.ts` — `toFrac()` turns `from`/`to` strings into fractional years (year-only starts count as September, ends as July; "heute" = build date, so rebuild to move it).
- `src/lib/heliostat.ts` — hero field geometry (`FIELD`, `MIRROR_X`, `SUN_START`, `poses()`, `clampSun()`, `fieldEfficiency()`), shared by server render and client script.
- `src/lib/amModel.ts` — AM model constants, presets, `amModel()`, `amMarks()`, `amSvg()`, `amVerdict()`, formatters.
- `src/components/` — `Placeholder`, `ImagePlaceholder`, `sections/Werdegang`, `sections/Kenntnisse`, `sections/Kontakt`, `figures/HeliostatField`, `figures/Heliostat` (reflection schematic, in the Bachelorarbeit station), `figures/LifeCycle` and `figures/AmModel` (unused while Projekte is out).
- `src/layouts/Layout.astro` — shell: fonts, `html.js` one-liner, skip link, draft notice, top bar with the section scroll spy, `<main id="inhalt" class="content">`. Sections wrap their own content in `.wrap` so bands can go full-bleed.
- `src/pages/index.astro` — the only page: hero, Kernkompetenzen, then the three section components.
- JS on the site: the `html.js` one-liner, the hero field, the Kernkompetenzen stacking panels, the Werdegang scroll sync, the top bar scroll spy. Everything works without it.
- Section heads: Werdegang, Kenntnisse and Kontakt open with the `.display` title (h2), top padding 3rem at ≥64rem.
- CSS cascade caution: media-query overrides must come *after* the base rule they override (this broke the site twice); page-level overrides of layout/global rules need higher specificity (e.g. `:global(:root)`, `:global(main.content#inhalt)`) because Astro's scoped selectors carry an extra attribute.
